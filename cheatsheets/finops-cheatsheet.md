# Google Cloud FinOps Cheat Sheet (PCA Exam)

---

## What is FinOps?
- **FinOps = Cloud Financial Operations**
- Combines finance, engineering, and business to optimize cloud spend
- Focus: Visibility, accountability, and continuous cost optimization

---

## FinOps Principles
- Teams collaborate on cloud spend
- Everyone takes ownership for usage
- Reports and metrics are accessible and actionable
- Decisions are driven by business value
- Cloud usage is continuously optimized

---

## Key FinOps Practices
- **Visibility:** Use Billing, Budgets, and Cost Management tools
- **Allocation:** Tagging/labeling resources, project-level separation
- **Optimization:** Rightsizing, idle resource cleanup, Committed Use Discounts, Preemptible VMs
- **Forecasting:** Use historical data for budget planning
- **Automation:** Alerts, auto-shutdown, policy enforcement
- **Governance:** Set policies, review spend, enforce accountability

---

## GCP FinOps Tools
- **Billing Console:** Cost breakdowns, trends, and forecasts
- **Budgets & Alerts:** Set spend limits, get notified
- **Recommender:** Rightsizing, idle VM detection, cost-saving suggestions
- **Cost Table/Reports:** Detailed, filterable spend analysis
- **Labels/Tags:** Track spend by team, project, environment
- **Committed Use Discounts:** Save on predictable workloads
- **BigQuery Billing Export:** Advanced analysis and custom dashboards

---

## Best Practices
- Review spend regularly (weekly/monthly)
- Involve engineering, finance, and business in reviews
- Use automation for cleanup and alerts
- Educate teams on cost impact of design choices
- Continuously iterate: measure, optimize, repeat

---

**Exam Triggers:**
- Know GCP tools for cost visibility and optimization
- Apply FinOps principles to scenario questions (e.g., “How to reduce cloud spend?”)
- Recommend automation and governance for cost control
